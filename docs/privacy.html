Privacy notice

How we handle the details you enter into the Resident Registry.

Data controller

The Address Management Committee is the data controller for information you enter into this Registry. To raise questions, request correction, or request deletion, write to us at loading….

What we store

  • Flat identity (tower, flat number, occupancy, move-in date, prior address).
  • Primary resident (name, DOB, mobile, email).
  • Family members (name, relation, DOB, optional mobile).
  • Vehicles (type, registration, colour, parking slot).
  • Emergency contact.

In v1 we do not collect photos, KYC documents (Aadhaar / PAN / rental agreement), domestic-help details, or pets. These may be added later after a separate committee review and update to this notice.

Where it lives

Records are stored in a private GitHub repository (tadeskops/trr_record) owned by the committee. Only administrators and Resident Registry Managers with a signed-in session can view records; residents can only see their own.

Access is protected by email one-time-codes. No password is ever stored or transmitted.

Your rights

You may correct or delete your record at any time — write to the Committee at the email address listed on the noticeboard, or click Delete my data from your household page (coming soon). Move-out flats are anonymised: identifying fields are cleared and only the occupancy history remains.

Who we do NOT share with

We do not sell, trade, or share resident data with vendors, marketing partners, or third parties. Data may be used, in aggregate and anonymised form, for community planning (e.g. "how many EVs are registered").
