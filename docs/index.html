What we collect & why

  • Flat identity, occupancy status, move-in date.
  • Primary resident: name, DOB, mobile, email.
  • Family members (name, relation, DOB, mobile).
  • Vehicles (type, registration, colour).
  • One emergency contact.

Data stays on the Committee's private repository, is never sold or shared with third parties, and you can request deletion at any time. Read the full privacy notice.
